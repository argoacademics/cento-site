Every archive begins with a refusal — the refusal to let a phrase dissolve once the page is closed. Something is carried forward: a hem of the page caught between two fingers, held up to the light. [[SS-0003/SS-0003|SS-0003]] is the first source text to enter this graph, a work that has travelled far enough to leave a mark in more than one margin. From it, [[SS-0001/SS-0001|SS-0001]] and [[SS-0002/SS-0002|SS-0002]] extend outward, each bearing the trace of a stitch — a phrase extracted, transplanted, and made to root in foreign soil.
